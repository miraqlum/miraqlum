# Usage, daily limits and monthly bonuses

MiraQlum measures meaningful WordPress work through your connected account. The product uses a daily allowance, may offer an eligible Welcome period and optional account-bound bonuses, and applies fair-use protections to resource-intensive activity.

**For current allowances, bonus quantities, eligibility, billing periods, price offers and fair-use terms, see the [official MiraQlum Plans page](https://operator.miraqlum.com/plans/).** The current applicable offer — not a dated GitHub snapshot or an installed plugin README — governs the displayed plan information. Consumer checkout must show the applicable taxes and amount payable before payment.

## How usage works

- Basic account/connection status, site listing, relevant capability discovery, basic planning and status of previously accepted jobs are available under the current no-charge control policy, including when the daily work balance is exhausted.
- A separately authorized, accepted, bounded WordPress change-set is accounted for once. Rechecking its status or retrying internally does **not** independently consume another unit.
- Fresh, user-requested work can consume allowance even when a requested operation yields no content change, depending on the published usage policy.
- Resource-intensive autonomous analysis and broad inspection are subject to separate, disclosed fair-use safeguards; **not every possible inspection is unlimited**.
- Your sites share one connected account's relevant allowance; there is no per-WordPress-site marketing price tier in the documented product approach.

The precise counted operation and current entitlement come from the live MiraQlum service, not the number of AI tokens or individual MCP tool calls.

## Welcome and bonuses

Eligible new accounts can receive a Welcome allowance beginning at their first verified WordPress-site connection. Reconnecting a site or linking another site does not automatically restart it. Optional monthly bonuses require an explicit user action; the service reports their current status and expiry. The applicable duration and amounts are always those shown in the live service and current official offer.

## Ask your connected assistant

> Using only my MiraQlum Operator connection, show daily usage, remaining allowance, reset time, available and activated bonuses, and usage by connected WordPress site. Present it as a clear visual dashboard if supported; otherwise use a table. Do not activate any bonuses.

Your authenticated MiraQlum connection provides account-scoped usage and reset information. ChatGPT and Claude can display the response differently; in particular, a contextual notice is not a guarantee of an email, push notification or clickable button. Bonus activation requires your explicit instruction.

## When your allowance is exhausted

New chargeable WordPress work may be declined until the daily allowance renews or an eligible bonus is activated. Basic permitted account/status/support views remain available. Previously accepted authorized work can still complete and be verified.

Daily and monthly boundaries are currently expressed by the service in UTC, which may not match local midnight. Check your own returned reset and expiry times rather than assuming a local-time date.

## Prices and paid availability

Paid subscription purchase and Paddle-derived entitlements are **not yet presented here as live**. If paid plans become available, current terms and pre-purchase final totals will be shown on the official service website and relevant checkout. A different AI client may have its own independent subscription and usage costs.
